Stress Detection

Heart rate, skin response, motion, and temperature already separate the two labels. Three tree models land in the same place.

  1. 01 · The tableBVP, EDA, acceleration on three axes, temperature, and a stress label. About 480,000 rows.

Question

  • Can this sensor bundle tell a stressed window from a calm one?

Why this path

  • A linear model is the check that the signal is real. If only a huge net worked, the table would be suspicious.
  • Three ensembles within a point of each other means the data is carrying the result.

What held

~93%logistic regression
~98%forest, XGBoost, and LightGBM
1 pointbetween the three tree models

Reading the result

  • Forest about 98.2%, LightGBM about 98.3%, XGBoost about 98.4%. F1 for those three stays above 0.98.
  • The linear model is the one that falls behind, and it is still in the low 90s, so the sensors are informative on their own.
  • LightGBM or the forest is the practical choice. Another boosting library does not move the decision.

What the notebook keeps

rows 480,052 signals BVP, EDA, ACC, TEMP label 0 non-stress 1 stress curves ROC already executed

Why the scores sit together

  • Once the window is labeled and the sensors agree, most reasonable trees find the same cut.
  • The figures are saved in the notebook, so the ranking can be read without training again.

Scope. One device and one labeling scheme. A different wristband would be a new fit. The table stays on this machine.

scikit-learn, Random Forest, XGBoost, LightGBM. The notebook is the artifact.
